Who named them?

A 9-year-old girl named Sofi Collis chose “Spirit” and “Opportunity” in a contest. People call Opportunity “Oppy”.

NASA fact

Each rover is about 1.6 m long, about the size of a golf cart.

      About this project

      Twin Rover Quest is a student project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Every fact sits in a “NASA fact” box with a source. Robot voices are imagined and labelled.

      Silica is a clue about water, not proof of life. NASA has not reviewed or endorsed this project.

      Privacy

      Progress is kept in this browser only. No names, no accounts, nothing is sent anywhere.
